Is Gmail depressed, or is it just the open rates? Email senders are reporting major drops in Gmail open rates over the past few months—some Validity customers are seeing quarter-over-quarter drops of 30 percent or more. Validity’s own engagement data confirms it: Gmail image loading activity—including tracking pixels—dropped by roughly one third in late November 2025.
