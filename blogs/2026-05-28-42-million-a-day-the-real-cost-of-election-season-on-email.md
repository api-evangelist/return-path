During high-volume sending periods, inbox placement isn’t just determined by how good your own email program is—it’s shaped by everything happening in the ecosystem around you. Take Black Friday through Cyber Monday, for example. This is one of the highest-volume sending periods of the year, and mailbox providers (MBPs) hit capacity limits fast.
