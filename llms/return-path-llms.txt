# Validity

> Whether you're looking to optimize the performance of your email marketing, data management or sales functions, Validity is your trusted partner to ensure you're reaching who you need to.

For over 20 years, tens of thousands of organizations throughout the world have relied on Validity solutions to target, contact, engage, and keep customers using trustworthy data as a key advantage. Founded in 2018 and headquartered in Boston with offices in London, Tampa, and São Paulo, Validity has grown through strategic acquisitions to become a global leader in data integrity. The company's flagship products – DemandTools, BriteVerify, Everest, GridBuddy Connect, Litmus, and MailCharts – are all highly rated solutions for CRM data management, email address verification, inbox deliverability, and grid CRM applications that help organizations drive more qualified leads, close more deals, and achieve faster growth with clean, actionable data.

## Products

- [BriteVerify](https://www.validity.com/briteverify/): Industry's longest-standing contact verification solution providing secure, scalable email validation to build and maintain an actionable database, reach more people, and communicate more effectively.
- [DemandTools](https://www.validity.com/demandtools/): Versatile and secure data management platform that enables organizations to clean and manage Salesforce data in less time, enabling everyone to do their jobs more effectively, efficiently, and profitably.
- [Everest](https://www.validity.com/everest/): Comprehensive email deliverability platform providing crucial insights and guidance to reach more subscriber inboxes, created by the most respected pioneers of email optimization and deliverability.
- [GridBuddy Connect](https://www.validity.com/gridbuddyconnect/): Data productivity platform that transforms how teams interact with data through versatile grids, creating a clear, informative way to access insights in one place for more productive end users.
- [Litmus](https://www.litmus.com/): Powerful tool that works alongside your ESP to help you create, personalize, and test your way to error-free emails in less time, ensuring your subscribers receive flawless communications.
- [MailCharts](https://www.mailcharts.com/): Campaign intelligence platform that gives digital marketers industry insights and inspiration to create email and SMS communications that capture attention and grow sales.
- [Sender Certification](https://www.validity.com/sender-certification/): Email deliverability insurance policy that helps get more emails to the inbox by protecting sender reputation with major providers like Google, Microsoft, Yahoo, and Apple.
